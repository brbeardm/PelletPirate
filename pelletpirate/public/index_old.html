Setup Pellet Pirate

Connecting the controller to WiFi.

  1. Plug-in your controller.
  2. Make sure it is blinking blue. If it isn't, press and hold the 'S' button until it does.
  3. Search your wireless networks and connect to the new WiFi hotspot created by Pellet Pirate. It will appear as Photon-XXXX on your network list.
  4. Press Next.
