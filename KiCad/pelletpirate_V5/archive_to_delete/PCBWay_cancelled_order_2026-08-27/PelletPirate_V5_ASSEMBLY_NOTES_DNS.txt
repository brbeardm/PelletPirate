SUBSTITUTION POLICY FOR ALL OTHER LINES
---------------------------------------
Generic passives and connectors not listed above may be quoted with
alternatives of EQUAL OR BETTER specification (tolerance, voltage rating,
temperature rating, package). Every proposed alternative must appear in the
BOM match report for customer approval BEFORE production. Operating
environment is outdoor: -40C minimum rating on any substitute.

ASSEMBLY CAUTIONS
-----------------
1. FPC1 (bottom side): 0.3mm-pitch staggered two-row connector. Verify paste
   and placement; no solder mask dams exist between its pads by design.
2. J8 vertical USB-C: 16 SMT pads + 4 through-hole shield slots. Shield slots
   must be soldered (mechanical retention for cable insertion loads).
3. J9 (TB002-500-02BE screw terminal): hand or wave solder ONLY per
   manufacturer datasheet - no reflow. Same for other through-hole parts.
4. T1-T3 (TO-220 triacs): mount vertical, fully seated per silkscreen.
5. Polarized parts: C7, C13, C15 electrolytics and D1, D2, LED1 - orientation
   per silkscreen. Note D1 polarity mark is a dot, not a band.
6. F1 is the fuse HOLDER only (Schurter 0031.8201) - no fuse cartridge is to
   be installed at assembly.
7. WirePad1-6 and Earth1 are bare solder-wire terminals - no components.
8. TP1/TP2 are bare test points - no components.
9. Drill file includes 25 plated slots (G85) around PS1 - these are creepage
   slots, fabricate as routed slots.
10. Position file origin: absolute board coordinates, millimeters, both sides.
