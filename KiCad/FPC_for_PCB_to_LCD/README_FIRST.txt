REV B - OPENING INSTRUCTIONS

1. Open PCB_to_LCD_FPC.kicad_pro in KiCad.
2. PCB_to_LCD_FPC.kicad_pcb is a modern KiCad board file.
3. The schematic is intentionally supplied as KiCad legacy .sch plus its cache library.
   KiCad 9/10 can open legacy schematics and converts them to .kicad_sch when saved.
4. Open PCB_to_LCD_FPC.sch in Schematic Editor, then Save As / Save. KiCad will create
   PCB_to_LCD_FPC.kicad_sch.
5. The schematic shows J1 and J2 as 39-pin connectors with straight-through 1:1 nets.
6. PRELIMINARY: physically verify pin-1/contact-side orientation before ordering.

Connector: Hirose FH26W-39S-0.3SHW(60)
Flex: 75 mm overall, 14 mm connector head, 12 mm tail, 0.3 mm pitch, 39 positions.
