1. BOARD SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Layers ............. 2 (copper top + bottom)
  Board size ......... 30.0 mm x 70.0 mm
  Thickness .......... 1.6 mm
  Material ........... FR-4
  Copper weight ...... 1 oz
  Surface finish ..... ENIG  (REQUESTED - see note in Section 6)
  Min track/space .... ~0.20 mm / 0.15 mm (standard)
  Min hole ........... 0.30 mm
  Drill files ........ PTH and NPTH provided SEPARATELY.
                       The 2x non-plated 0.65 mm holes are USB-C locating
                       pegs and MUST remain non-plated (see NPTH drill file).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ASSEMBLY SCOPE  (READ FIRST - MIXED TECHNOLOGY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  This board is MIXED-TECHNOLOGY. Quote/assemble BOTH processes:

  A) SMT (reflow), TOP side:
       ESP1, U1, J8 (signal pads), LED1,
       C17, C18, C37, C38, C39, C40,
       R30, R31, R35, R36, R37, R38, R39

  B) THROUGH-HOLE (hand or selective solder) - DO NOT SKIP:
       J1, J2  ... 20-pin pin headers, mounted on the BOTTOM side
       S1, S2  ... Omron B3F tactile switches
       J8      ... USB-C is HYBRID: SMD signal pads (top) PLUS through-hole
                   shield/ground posts. Solder the THT shield posts.

  NOTE: A default "SMT only" quote will MISS the through-hole parts.
        Please include through-hole soldering in the quote.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SIDE ASSIGNMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TOP  .... all SMD components (see 2A)
  BOTTOM .. J1 and J2 pin headers (pins protrude downward for breadboard use)
            >> Do NOT flip J1/J2 to the top side. <<

7. SPECIAL PROCESS NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - ESP1 has an exposed thermal pad (EP / pad 41) on its underside, with a
    via array to the bottom ground plane. Apply correct paste volume; ensure
    good EP reflow without bridging to adjacent perimeter pads.
  - USB-C (J8) signal pads are 0.5 mm pitch (fine pitch). ENIG finish is
    requested to improve coplanarity and assembly yield.
  - There are NO do-not-populate (DNP) parts. Populate all references.
